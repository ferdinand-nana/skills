# Peer Evaluation Template

This file defines the structure of the peer evaluation extracted from the xls file. Peer evaluation is consist of 5 questions, each question is covering the following topics:
1. Work Quality & Improvement: Can you share any examples where my work stood out—either positively or where it could have been better? What could I do to improve?
2. Communication & Collaboration: How well do I communicate and collaborate with the team? Are there any situations where I could have been clearer or more helpful?
3. Reliability & Accountability: Do I consistently deliver on my responsibilities and own up to mistakes? Please share any examples that come to mind.
4. Growth & Technical Skills: What skills or areas should I focus on to grow as a developer? Have you noticed any improvements since last year?
5. Team Culture & Support: How do I contribute to a positive team environment? What more could I do to support team morale and camaraderie?

Now, each question maps to the 3 parts in the self-evaluation template:
1. The WHAT: What were the key achievements and overall performance during the period?
2. The HOW: How were the achievements accomplished? What behaviors and skills were demonstrated?
3. Your GROWTH: What personal development and growth occurred during the period?

Here's the mapping of the questions to the 3 parts in the self-evaluation template:
| Question Title | The WHAT | The HOW | Your GROWTH |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Work Quality & Improvement | ✓ |  | ✓ |
| Communication & Collaboration |  | ✓ |  |
| Reliability & Accountability |  | ✓ | ✓ |
| Growth & Technical Skills |  |  | ✓ |
| Team Culture & Support |  | ✓ | ✓ |


Here's the self-evaluation template:

Filename should be in the format of `Peer_Eval_{Name}.md` where {Name} is the name of the peer being evaluated. For example, `Peer_Eval_Hazel_Mendoza.md`.
