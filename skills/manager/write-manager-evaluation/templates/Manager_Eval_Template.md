# Manager Evaluation Format

This file defines the canonical output structure for the manager evaluation. Use this format to produce a balanced, evidence-backed, and manager-ready performance review.

## Output Template

```
Manager Evaluation — {period} — {name}, {role}

---

## The WHAT — Performance Summary

[Put the overall performance level here, e.g., "Overall, {name} consistently met expectations for their role as a {role} during {period}, with particular strengths in [key areas]. There are opportunities for growth in [development areas], which we will focus on in the coming period."]
<2–3 sentence summary of the employee's overall performance for the period.
State the performance level clearly (e.g., "consistently met expectations", "exceeded expectations in key areas").
Reference the role criteria from ROLE_DESCRIPTOR.md to anchor the assessment.>

### [Section title for primary achievement area]
<Describe the employee's key accomplishments as observed outcomes. Use metrics and business impact where available.
Do not copy from the self-evaluation — reframe achievements from the manager's perspective.
For Manager-role employees, focus on team delivery results and leadership impact over individual technical contributions.>

### [Section title for secondary achievement area, if applicable]
<Additional achievement narrative.>

### Challenges and How They Were Handled
<Describe any significant obstacles the employee faced. Acknowledge how they were navigated.
Draw from both the self-evaluation and peer feedback for evidence.>

**<Finastra Behavior #1>**
<2–3 sentences describing how the employee demonstrated this behavior, with specific examples from observed outcomes or peer feedback.>

**<Finastra Behavior #2>**
<2–3 sentences describing how the employee demonstrated this behavior.>

**<Finastra Behavior #3>** *(optional)*
<2–3 sentences describing how the employee demonstrated this behavior.>
```

## Tone & Length Guidance
- Write in first person from the manager's perspective ("I observed...", "The team delivered...", "Ferdinand demonstrated...")
- Professional, direct, and balanced - avoid overly generic praise or harsh criticism and not sound AI-generated
- Use specific examples and evidence to support statements about performance and behaviors
- Keep it 2 - 3 paragraphs for "The WHAT", 2 - 3 paragraphs for "The HOW", and 3 - 4 paragraphs for "Your GROWTH", a total of 600 to 800 words.
- Lead with impact, not activity - say what was achieved, not just what was done
- Where peer feedback corroborates or contrasts the self-assessment, weave it in naturally without quoting verbatim
- For Manager-role employees: emphasize team outcomes, culture, and leadership over individual technical work
- STRICTLY DO NOT use "—" or dash or hypens in the paragraphs because it feels like AI generated; use "-" only for bullet points.
