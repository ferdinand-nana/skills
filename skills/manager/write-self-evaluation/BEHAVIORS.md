# Finastra Behaviors
This documents defines the core behaviors that Finastra values and expects from its employees. These behaviors are aligned with the company values and are used as a framework for performance evaluation, development, and recognition.

## Customer Centric
Means putting our customers and colleagues at the heart of everything we do. We listen to their needs, understand their challenges, act with empathy, and deliver solutions that create value for them and our community. We unlock the power of open finance by creating bridges between people and businesses - removing trade finance barriers, boosting financial inclusion and enabling innovation.

### High Performance Indicators
- Goes beyond expectations to resolve a customer complaint​
- Finds an opportunity to improve the customer experience​
- Dedicated and committed to their work, going above and beyond in terms of quality and impact of their contributions

### Common Behaviors
- "I build and maintain strong relationships"
- "I am proactive at finding solutions"
- "I strive to excel in ​everything I do"



## Stronger Together
Means leveraging our diversity, our global presence, and our collective strengths. We work as one team, across functions, geographies, and cultures. We support each other, learn from each other, and celebrate our successes together.

### High Performance Indicators
- Asks everyone for their input in a team conversation, ensuring all members feel included​
- Shares their expertise openly with others​
- Consistently exceed expectations and deliver outstanding results, surpassing KPIs and setting a high standard for others

### Common Behaviors
- "I encourage diverse views and perspectives"
- "I work openly and communicate with others to achieve our goals​"
- "I celebrate our successes​"



## Growth Mindset
Means embracing change, innovation, and continuous improvement. We are curious, creative, and resilient. We seek feedback, learn from our mistakes, and challenge ourselves to grow personally and professionally.

### High Performance Indicators
- Seeks feedback after completing a project​
- Asks to take on a new challenge to develop new skills​
- Always looking to learn new skills and take on more responsibilities​
- Shares feedback with empathy and is open to constructive criticism in return

### Common Behaviors
- "I continually grow, share ​and develop"
- "I actively look for better ways ​of doing things​"
- "I learn from experiences and adjust accordingly​"



## Shared Accountability
Means taking ownership of our actions, decisions, and results. We set clear goals, measure our progress, and deliver on our commitments. We act with integrity, transparency, and courage. We hold ourselves and each other accountable for excellence.

### High Performance Indicators
- Takes ownership regardless of the end results​
- Solves a problem even though it isn’t part of their job or doesn’t directly impact them​
- Actively seeks out opportunities to innovate and improve processes

### Common Behaviors
- "I make informed and ​timely decisions​"
- "I take ownership and ​lead by example​"
- "I own my contributions ​and outcomes​"
