# Self-Evaluation Format

This file defines the canonical output structure the skill generates. Use this format to produce manager-ready, concise, and evidence-backed self-evaluations.

Here's the self-evaluation template:

<self-evaluation-template>
Self-evaluation — {period} — {name}, {role}

The WHAT
- Highlight the key achievements in the period, with metrics and impact. Use concise bullets or a short narrative depending on the provided information.
- Provide a summary of the overall performance, referencing the chosen role and how the achievements align with the role criteria. The summary must be 2–4 sentences, with a clear statement of the overall assessment.
- Add section titles whenever necessary to improve readability and structure.


The HOW
- Map the achievements to the behaviors defined in [BEHAVIORS.md](BEHAVIORS.md) to demonstrate how the employee embodied the Finastra values in their work.
- Choose 2-3 behaviors that are most relevant to the achievements and provide specific examples of how they were demonstrated.
- Use the format:
<Finastra Behavior #1>
<Description of how the behavior was demonstrated with specific examples from the achievements or how the behavior contributed to the results in 2-3 sentences only.>

<Finastra Behavior #2>
<Description of how the behavior was demonstrated with specific examples from the achievements or how the behavior contributed to the results in 2-3 sentences only.>

<Finastra Behavior #3>
<Description of how the behavior was demonstrated with specific examples from the achievements or how the behavior contributed to the results in 2-3 sentences only.>
- Should be reflective and evidence-based, with a focus on how the behaviors contributed to the achievements and overall performance. Use specific examples to illustrate the connection between behaviors and results.


Your GROWTH
- Reflect on the personal development goals and aspirations provided by the user. Highlight any opportunities taken to learn something new or do something different, and how these contributed to growth.
- Use the [TOP_REQUIRED_SKILLS.md](TOP_REQUIRED_SKILLS.md) to understand the key skills that are relevant to the user's role and aspirations. Map any achievements or behaviors to these skills to demonstrate growth in these areas or highlight growth areas that the user is actively working on.
- Provide a summary of the growth and development, referencing any specific skills or areas of development that were highlighted by the user. This should be 2-3 sentences that tie the growth back to the user's aspirations and the overall performance.
- Should be forward-looking and reflective.

</self-evaluation-template>


## Tone & length guidance
- Professional but natural, Leadership-oriented for Manager and Technical Leader role, Honest and balanced, Technical yet understandable, evidence-forward, 800 - 1000 words only.
- Make sure the self-evaluation represents the user and NOT an AI-generated, AI-soundy self-evaluation.
- Make the self-evaluation concise but impactful. Sentences should be clear and direct and not too long and make the ideas easy to understand.

## Writing Approach
- Use storytelling instead of dry bullet points
- Present achievements as a journey across the year
- Show technical contribution whenever applicable
- Show leadership impact for Manager and Technical Leader and Senior Software Engineer role
- Highlight challenges and how they were resolved
- Balance accomplishments with areas for growth
- Emphasize collaboration
- Emphasize mentorship for Manager and Technical Leader and Senior Software Engineer role whenever applicable
- Use simple language to explain technical achievements, especially for Manager role
