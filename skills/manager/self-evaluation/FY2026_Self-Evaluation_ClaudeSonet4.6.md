# Self-evaluation — FY2026 — Ferdinand Nana, Manager

## The WHAT

### Driving Strategic Deliveries

FY2026 was a year of significant delivery impact for the team, anchored by two major initiatives: the Payment Factory and the Reports Cargo Spring Migration.

The **Payment Factory** represented a substantial enhancement to Finastra's payments capabilities, supporting multiple standard formats critical to international payments. The team delivered end-to-end support for Pain 001 v3, v3ch02, v9, and MT101 payment formats, underpinned by four foundational capabilities: File Exchange, Configuration, Workflow, and Transmission via Service Bureau. This complete delivery positions our product to meet the evolving regulatory and operational demands of clients navigating complex cross-border payment landscapes.

The **Reports Cargo Spring Migration** was a broad-scope platform modernization effort that required the team to move methodically across a large surface area. The team successfully migrated Components Services, Reports Services—encompassing Workspace Features, Tenant Management, User Management, Snapshot Management, Bookmarks, and Capture Templates—as well as the Report Designer Service and Configuration Service to SpringBoot. This migration reduces technical debt, improves long-term maintainability, and aligns the platform with Finastra's strategic technology direction.

Alongside these major deliveries, the team identified and resolved high-severity security vulnerabilities, demonstrating our commitment to product resilience and client trust.

### Personal Technical Contributions

While steering the team through these deliveries, I remained actively engaged on the technical front. I refactored the CMS implementation to align with updated business requirements, ensuring correctness and maintainability as the solution evolved. I led the implementation of authentication across the SpringBoot applications, establishing a secure and reusable pattern that the broader team could build upon. I also created automation scripts to standardize application setup in both local and server environments, significantly reducing onboarding friction and improving developer productivity. Finally, I delivered the Configuration Service Application—a foundational piece that centralizes property management across all SpringBoot services, underpinning the stability of the entire migrated stack.

Together, these team and personal contributions reflect a year of consistent, high-impact execution. As a Manager, I led a team through two complex, cross-functional deliveries while staying technically grounded—demonstrating the balance of leadership, accountability, and business alignment that defines the Manager grade.
