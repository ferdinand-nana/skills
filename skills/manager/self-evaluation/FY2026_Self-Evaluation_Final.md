# Self-evaluation — FY2026 — Ferdinand Nana, Manager

## The WHAT

### Driving Strategic Deliveries

FY2026 was a strong delivery year for the team. We focused on two major initiatives: the Payment Factory implementation and the Reports Cargo Spring Migration.

The **Payment Factory** was a major upgrade to CTI's payments capabilities. The team delivered support for PAIN.001 v3, PAIN.001 v3ch02, PAIN.001 v9, and MT101 payment formats, covering four core capabilities: File Exchange, Configuration, Workflow, and Transmission via Service Bureau. This delivery required close coordination across product management, development, and QA to ensure everything came together as one cohesive solution. It was complex work, and the team executed it well.

The **Reports Cargo Spring Migration** was a large-scale modernization of CTI's reporting platform. The team migrated the Reports Cargo Service into four Spring Boot services:
(1) Components Service
(2) Reports Services (including Workspace Features, Tenant Management, User Management, Snapshot Management, Bookmarks, and Capture Templates)
(3) Report Designer Service and 
(4) Configuration Service
This was a careful migration where the team made significant technical changes while preserving continuity for all supported features. The result is a leaner, more maintainable platform aligned with our long-term technology direction.

Alongside these deliveries, the team identified and resolved high-severity security vulnerabilities, keeping the product safe and reliable for our clients.

### Personal Technical Contributions

While leading the team through these deliveries, I stayed hands-on technically. I refactored the CMS implementation to align with updated requirements. I helped implement authentication in the Spring Boot applications, setting a consistent security pattern across all migrated services. I also built automation scripts for application setup in local and server environments, and delivered the Configuration Service, the component that manages properties for all the other Spring Boot services.

FY2026 was a year where the team delivered on its commitments and I stayed close to the work. Leading complex deliveries while remaining technically involved is something I take seriously as KTP Manila's Development Manager, and this year showed that balance is achievable.
