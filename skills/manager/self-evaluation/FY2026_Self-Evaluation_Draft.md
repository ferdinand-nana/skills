# Self-evaluation — FY2026 — Ferdinand Nana, Manager

## The WHAT

### Driving Strategic Deliveries

FY2026 was a year of significant delivery impact for the team, anchored by two major initiatives: the Payment Factory implementation and the Reports Cargo Spring Migration.

The **Payment Factory** implementation represented a substantial enhancement to CTI's payments capabilities, supporting multiple standard formats critical to international payments. The team delivered end-to-end support for PAIN.001 v3, PAIN.001 v3ch02, PAIN.001 v9, and MT101 payment formats, underpinned by four foundational capabilities: File Exchange, Configuration, Workflow, and Transmission via Service Bureau. This complete delivery positions our product to meet the evolving regulatory and operational demands of clients navigating complex cross-border payments. This delivery was particularly complex because it required coordination across product management, development, and QA to ensure the solution came together as a coherent product rather than a set of disconnected features. The successful execution of this initiative is a testament to the team's ability to manage complexity and deliver high-impact solutions that align with business needs.

The **Reports Cargo Spring Migration** was a broad-scope platform modernization effort that required the team to move methodically across a large surface area. The team successfully migrated the Reports Cargo Service to multiple SpringBoot based services: 
(1) Components Service
(2) Reports Services (including Workspace Features, Tenant Management, User Management, Snapshot Management, Bookmarks, and Capture Templates)
(3) Report Designer Service and 
(4) Configuration Service
This work represented a meaningful modernization step for CTI and required the team to manage technical change carefully while still preserving continuity for the supported features and services. In addition, the migration reduces technical debt, improves long-term maintainability, and aligns the platform with the business' strategic technology direction.

Alongside these major deliveries, the team identified and resolved high-severity security vulnerabilities, demonstrating our commitment to product resilience and client trust.

### Personal Technical Contributions

While steering the team through these deliveries, I remained actively engaged on the technical front. I refactored the CMS implementation to respond to updated requirements. I also supported the implementation of authentication in Spring Boot applications, which contributed to more consistent security foundations across the services being modernized. I created automations for application setup in both local and server environments. And implemented the Configuration Service application that manages properties for the other Spring Boot services.

Together, these team and personal contributions reflect a year of consistent, high-impact execution. As a Manager, I led a team through two complex, cross-functional deliveries while staying technically grounded—demonstrating the balance of leadership, accountability, and business alignment that defines my role as KTP Manila Development Manager.
